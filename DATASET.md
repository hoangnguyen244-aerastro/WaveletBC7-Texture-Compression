# Dataset provenance and splits

The repository currently contains Brick, Metal and Wood texture sets, each with
albedo, normal and roughness maps under `resources/textures/train/`.

The **source, licensing and permission for redistribution are not documented**.
These details must be confirmed before publishing the dataset in a thesis or release.

The first benchmark targets albedo RGB only. Do not claim independent
validation/test performance from these training samples. Add separately sourced
textures and document a material-level split before drawing general conclusions.
